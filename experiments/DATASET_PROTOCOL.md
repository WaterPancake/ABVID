## 1. Scope and freeze boundary

The core task is closed-set, single-event classification into **car and truck**, using public civilian recordings. These are broad categories, not vehicle models or physical identities. There is no background output, vehicle-presence claim, congestion classifier, or military-model experiment. Existing ABVID training, demo and reserved recordings are excluded entirely. Existing tracked/wheeled synthetic profiles must not be relabelled as these civilian categories. Motorcycle is excluded from every core training, validation and test role in E0/E1/E2/E3, with exclusion reason `class_out_of_scope_v1.1`; raw files are retained. E2-P-R/S remain separate historical three-class snapshot replays, with no new fitting, and are not numerically comparable to the binary core.

The [September 28 metadata audit](../reports/dataset_readiness_audit.md) established filename counts and IDMT/AI4TEN header readability. R0 subsequently decoded selected raw waveforms and scored historical evaluation features; its checks do not certify the whole corpus. Admission steps 1–2 materialized a full inventory and decoded integrity/class manifest: 46,775 WAVs audited and 22,873 car/truck files passing. See the [results report](../reports/dataset_admission_integrity.md) and [v1.2.1 summary](admission/results/integrity_20261002_v1_2_1/summary.json). Those historical rows remain `integrity_and_class_pass`, not training admissions. The separate v1.3.1 manifest supplies qualified H1 roles and known-link grouping; it never rewrites those snapshots.

The historical steps 1–2 audit could automatically decode complete candidate waveforms, calculate hashes and test finite/nonzero samples, duration, channels and container consistency. Clipping and quiet levels are flags, not performance-based exclusions. It did not compute model embeddings or predictions. The subsequent authorized provenance pass computes fixed waveform fingerprints for reuse checks; H1 modelling starts only after the new lock. The full historical P01 MELAUDIS feature set was already scored: covered groups are exposed development data even where raw audio was not listened to. No untouched MELAUDIS confirmation partition is presently verified; metadata-only checks cannot restore one.

## 5. Eligibility and provenance units

Real core eligibility: valid decodable provider WAV (PCM or FLOAT); a provider-labelled event with usable grouping metadata; a single admissible class; provider two-second event duration; at least 2.000 s after resampling; nonzero finite waveform. Accept longer clips only by the fixed centre-crop rule in the experimental protocol. Exclude short clips rather than class-dependent padding. Keep clipping, quiet vehicles, wet roads and difficult backgrounds; they are not score-based exclusions. A corrupted file is excluded by the same rule before splitting, with reason recorded.

For MELAUDIS apply the additional `FF & 1V` filter above. This controls annotated multiplicity, not a guarantee that all distant interference is absent. For IDMT use annotated vehicle passings, not background excerpts. Use only provider-labelled sE8 car/truck events with the `SE_CH34` selector above. The observed motorcycle `SE_CH12` anomaly is outside this core task; it is not relabelled or repaired.

Build connected provenance groups **before filtering or cropping**. Join every pair of files sharing an original media recording, documented recording session, duplicate event, synchronized device capture or overlap in source time. Conservatively union all captures with the same dataset/site/calendar-date, across sensors and contiguous original media. Missing date: use the entire documented session; missing session/date: use the whole site. Missing all three: quarantine. A filename or two-second event is never itself sufficient evidence of an independent session.

Across different datasets, match original media IDs/URLs and exact decoded-audio hashes. For near duplicates, a data custodian may compute deterministic fingerprints solely to identify overlap; none of those features enter modelling. Put cross-dataset overlap groups in quarantine on both sides. All known synthetic descendants inherit their source/template family and all real derivatives inherit the known original/proxy grouping. For H1 only, unknown synthetic lineage is handled by keeping the entire bank in one training-only group (§6), without a synthetic holdout claim.

These rules measure unseen acquisition groups, not unseen physical vehicles, unless independent vehicle identity is actually documented. Unknown make/model identity stays unknown.

## 8. Authorized integrity and class/channel pass

Audit IDMT's complete WAV inventory, MELAUDIS's complete **vehicle** archive, and both complete released synthetic inventories. Retain out-of-scope files in the audit to preserve metadata relationships, but exclude them from the car/truck candidate manifest. The MELAUDIS background archive is outside this pass; it is not needed for the closed-set task. Provider labels are accepted as annotations, not independent human verification of audible vehicle identity.

Before decoding, save the inventory and these rules. Extract MELAUDIS to a separate work directory from a checksum-verified archive; retain archive-member paths and do not modify originals. Record byte-level SHA256 for every audited WAV. Decode the whole WAV to float64 without resampling, normalization, cropping or channel alteration. Define `decoded_audio_sha256` over a versioned sample-rate/channel/frame header plus frame-major little-endian float64 samples. This preserves PCM32 and FLOAT precision and detects exact decoded equality at the same native shape/rate; it is not a near-duplicate or resampling-invariant fingerprint.

Check RIFF/WAVE chunk bounds and declared payload length as well as decoder completion, because a decoder can accept a truncated WAV. Record decoded/header frame agreement, finite sample counts, exactly all-zero signals, zero channels, native duration, rate, subtype and channel count. Invalid container structure, failed/incomplete decoding, nonfinite samples, all-zero waveforms, zero mono downmix, or duration below two seconds prevent candidate eligibility. Unsupported formats are quarantined, not silently repaired. Native duration is the pre-resampling check; the later fixed resampler must still verify 32,000 samples at encoder input. Peak/near-full-scale counts are diagnostic flags only; retain quiet, clipped and difficult events. A near-full-scale count does not prove clipping.

Apply deterministic metadata rules:

- IDMT: exact C/T vehicle token; provider `SE` microphone; `CH34`; two decoded channels. Record both original channel IDs `[3,4]`, with future processing `mean(all_channels)`. Same recording timestamp/site and centered sample-position token establish a **candidate paired-event key** across sensors. Store sibling file IDs and label agreement; this is an acquisition link, not verified physical-vehicle identity or a completed session audit. Do not select array columns 3 and 4 from a two-column WAV.
- MELAUDIS: exact `FF` and `1V-Car`/`1V-Truck`. Keep LR/RL directions without a class-specific exception. Preserve lane/location/time tokens and compare filename mono/stereo with the decoded layout. A mono-labelled file with two bit-identical decoded channels is redundant mono and may pass, since averaging preserves it exactly; two distinct channels remain quarantined. Future processing averages the channels actually present. Out-of-range time tokens are preserved and quarantined without discarding independently parsed class/channel fields or guessing a corrected time. Location aliases and original sessions remain unresolved.
- Synthetic: exact car/truck folder and matching `ALDM_car/truck_<index>` or `PYROAD_car/truck_<index>` filename label, one decoded channel. Keep generator family as **release-labelled**, not independently verified backend. Verify the 201st AudioLDM car file's integrity and exact hash matches against the inventory; distinct bytes do not certify novel acoustic content or source independence.

Use separate `metadata_exclusion_reasons`, `integrity_errors`, `channel_errors`, `label_conflict_errors` and non-excluding `flags`. A row passing steps 1–2 is `integrity_and_class_pass`; all rows retain `admitted_for_training: false`, unknown final `provenance_group_id`, and `split_role: null`. Unresolved session/source lineage is a later blocker, not an invented value. Exact identical audio carrying incompatible provider annotations is a class ambiguity: quarantine every member of that equality group in step 2, without choosing a supposedly correct label. Same-label duplicate removal, near-duplicate resolution and connected-group completion remain in later step 3.

Outputs belong under `experiments/admission/results/<run_id>/`: pre-decode inventory, all-file audit JSONL, passing car/truck JSONL, exclusions JSONL, archive/source hashes, exact-equality groups, provider pair links, extra-car finding, software/config/script hashes, counts and a completion summary. The result summary is the authority for measured counts. Report results in `reports/dataset_admission_integrity.md`; never overwrite R0 or September 28 audit artifacts. A successful run is an audit snapshot, not a training dataset lock.

## 9. Provenance/reuse audit and qualified admission

[Audit code](admission/audit_provenance.py), [executed rules](admission/results/provenance_20261002_v1_3_1/rules_before_audit.json), [summary](admission/results/provenance_20261002_v1_3_1/summary.json) and [report](../reports/dataset_admission_provenance.md) make the remaining checks inspectable. Every indexed eligible-duration/integrity waveform was rehashed against the prior scan. No raw audio was modified.

The bounded reuse search downmixes and resamples each center 2 s to 2 kHz, demeans/L2-normalizes, hashes quantized values, and retrieves nearest projected-waveform candidates within and across datasets, including polarity reversal. A candidate must have absolute normalized correlation ≥.9999 and pass the same threshold on the full 8 kHz common-band center window. Exact native equality links are retained independently. These are waveform-equivalence checks, not learned embeddings, classification results, listening judgments or proof of arbitrary recording independence. Coverage excludes arbitrary shifted crops, heavy codec changes and time stretching. Record this limitation rather than claiming no possible overlap.

All 46,775 indexed files retain provenance relationships. The search fingerprinted 46,771 usable 2 s waveforms, compared 1,546,076 candidate pairs and verified 112 reuse edges; no cross-dataset matches were detected by this bounded search. The source-time sweep records 5,852 overlap components. Overlap connects groups but does not itself override labels. Equivalent waveforms with conflicting labels are quarantined; same-label duplicates retain one lexicographically ordered eligible member. This added 11 AudioLDM car/truck exclusions and one same-label MELAUDIS car exclusion beyond v1.2.1. Source groups are unioned through excluded files as well.

The preliminary v1.3 search is preserved; v1.3.1 formally adds 8 kHz verification. All 112 candidates passed and admissions are identical. This amendment preceded any H1 features, fits or predictions.
