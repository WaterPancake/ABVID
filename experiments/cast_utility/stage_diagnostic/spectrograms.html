CAST fit vs original: mel spectrograms

Each picture is the log-mel filterbank that BEATs computes from the 2 s clip before its transformer (128 bands, 25 ms frames, 10 ms hop), shown in dB with BEATs's normalization undone. Groups use the saved real-trained BEATs classifier's verdict on the fitted (smooth8, noise realization 0) re-render; the original is always classified correctly. The dashed line marks 4 kHz: neither version has content above it on this route.

Summary panels: left, the average spectrum; middle, how much the fit adds (+) or removes (−) per band; right, how much of the original's frame-to-frame variation the fit keeps (1 = all of it). Grey shading above 4 kHz is resampling-filter leakage, not signal. Below about 125 Hz a 25 ms frame cannot resolve the mel bands, so several bands repeat one FFT bin and the 78 Hz band is always empty (it shows as a dark row in the pictures and is omitted from the summaries). Descriptive training-data comparison only.
