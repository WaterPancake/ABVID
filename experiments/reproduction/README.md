# Active phase: published-result reproduction

The user redirected the work on 2026-09-28 to validating released data and reproducing the papers on **their original splits** before designing our own training splits. This phase takes precedence over the custom E0–E3 ordering in `sim_components_v1.1`. The car/truck-only study and its independent-group support floors are deferred, not prerequisites for these historical checks.

Initial targets are P01 (AI4TEN transformer study; Zenodo 22203993) and P02 (AI4TEN synthetic-to-real study; Zenodo 21264250), matching the supplied dataset families. P01's five original classes and P02's three original classes are retained, including motorcycle. IDMT's provider EUSIPCO-2021 split is a separate reference: it must not silently replace P01's published microphone-based partition.

Verification proceeds from archive checksums, file/schema/label alignment and release-code inspection, to recomputation of archived metrics, to inference with supplied checkpoints. A saved-results arithmetic check is not independent model reproduction. Checkpoint inference is not retraining and cannot reproduce training-seed variance. Exact retraining requires the original training inputs, trainer, split definitions, preprocessing and seed semantics; missing pieces are recorded rather than invented.

Published quirks remain explicit. No class removal, replacement source scaler, custom grouped split, model selection, hyperparameter search or target-driven repair belongs to this phase. Inspecting historical evaluation data does not preserve it as an untouched future target. The source releases are retained under `releases/`; new verification outputs go under `results/` and never overwrite author artifacts or raw data.

The [active report](../../reports/published_reproduction_status.md), updated 2026-09-29, records exact CNN, AST, BEATs and DANN matrices on both P01 domains, exact ArcFace on MELAUDIS, and one residual net-count difference for ArcFace on CH34. The earlier BEATs-family discrepancy was largely our replay's failure to advance NumPy's RNG during evaluation before balanced sampling. Use `replay_p01_beats_rng.py` for the corrected selection; the old transformer's BEATs path now requires an explicit historical-diagnostic flag.

The [current R0 ledger](results/R0_20260929/R0_result_ledger.json) verifies 18 current prediction artifacts and records 30 archived arithmetic rows, release inventories, limitations and missing training inputs. It supersedes the earlier [September 28 audit](results/reproduction_audit.json) for current status while retaining that history. The current-release checkpoint-audit pass is complete with a **partial reproduction** verdict: 17/18 matrices match, no training runs were reproduced, and P02's exact balanced selections/training engine remain absent. No later simulator ablation or new training protocol is authorized by this work.
